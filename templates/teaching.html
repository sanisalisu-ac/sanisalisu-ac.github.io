Teaching

Since 2019 I have taught undergraduate mathematics at Sule Lamido University and, more recently, in the open and distance learning programme of Iconic Open University. I teach analysis as a way of thinking, and computation as a way of checking that thinking.

Courses taught

  • Elementary Mathematics I, II and III

    Foundational calculus, algebra and coordinate geometry for first-year students.

  • Foundations of Mathematical Analysis I and II

    Sequences, limits, continuity and the completeness of the real line, with emphasis on writing proofs.

  • Lebesgue Measure and Integration

    Measure, measurable functions and the convergence theorems that make modern analysis work.

  • Mathematical Methods I

    Techniques from calculus and linear algebra used across the sciences.

  • Discrete Structures

    Logic, sets, relations, combinatorics and graphs for computing students.

Taught at Sule Lamido University Kafin Hausa and Iconic Open University, 2019 to the present.

Interests

Real and functional analysis, convex analysis and optimization, numerical analysis and numerical optimization, linear algebra, and computational mathematics with MATLAB and Python.

For students

Students interested in a project or thesis on iterative methods, fixed point theory or optimization are welcome to contact me with a short note on their background and interests. Good preparation includes real analysis, linear algebra and some programming in Python or MATLAB.

Free resources I recommend:

  • Convex Optimization by Boyd and Vandenberghe, with lecture slides and exercises.
  • MIT OpenCourseWare 18.100A, Real Analysis, lectures and notes.
  • CVXPY for modelling convex problems in Python.
  • Pymanopt for optimization on manifolds in Python.
  • The interactive examples on this site, which illustrate fixed point and projection methods.
