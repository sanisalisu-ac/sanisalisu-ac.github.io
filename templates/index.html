Sani Salisu

I work on numerical optimization and fixed point theory, moving between theory and computation. On the theoretical side, I establish the existence and uniqueness of solutions and study the properties of the operators involved. On the algorithmic side, I design and analyse iterative methods (proximal, splitting, projection, inertial and conjugate-gradient) for optimization problems, variational inequalities, monotone inclusions and fixed point problems. The work spans Hilbert spaces and nonlinear settings such as CAT(0)/Hadamard spaces and Riemannian manifolds, and extends to applications in supply-chain networks, machine learning, image restoration, robotics and portfolio selection.

  • Postdoctoral Researcher, School of Mathematical Sciences, Chongqing Normal University, China
  • Lecturer I, Department of IT & Software Engineering, Iconic Open University, Sokoto, Nigeria
