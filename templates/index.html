Sani Salisu

I study fixed points and the iterative methods that reach them: when a solution exists, what the operator around it looks like, and how to design algorithms that converge to it, in Hilbert spaces and well beyond them.

  • Postdoctoral Researcher, School of Mathematical Sciences, Chongqing Normal University, China
  • Lecturer I, Department of IT & Software Engineering, Iconic Open University, Sokoto, Nigeria

My work moves between theory and computation. On the theoretical side I prove existence and uniqueness results and study the operators involved, with particular attention to enriched and multivalued mappings in geodesic spaces. On the algorithmic side I design proximal, splitting, projection, inertial and conjugate-gradient methods, and test them on problems from supply-chain logistics, machine learning, image restoration, robotics and finance.
