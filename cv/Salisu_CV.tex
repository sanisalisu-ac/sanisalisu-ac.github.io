\cvsection{Research Profile}
My research lies in numerical optimization and fixed point theory, and moves between theory and computation. On the theoretical side, I establish the existence and uniqueness of solutions and study the properties of the operators involved. On the algorithmic side, I design and analyse iterative methods (proximal, splitting, projection, inertial and conjugate-gradient) for optimization problems, variational inequalities, monotone inclusions and fixed point problems. The work spans Hilbert spaces and nonlinear settings such as CAT(0)/Hadamard spaces and Riemannian manifolds, and extends to computation, with applications in supply-chain networks, machine learning, image restoration, robotics and portfolio selection.

%% ---------------------------- INTERESTS -----------------------------
\cvsection{Research Interests}
Numerical and nonsmooth optimization; variational inequalities, monotone inclusions and equilibrium problems; splitting, proximal and conjugate-gradient methods, including Riemannian and vector optimization; fixed point theory in Banach, metric and geodesic spaces (CAT(0), Hadamard, hyperbolic), with emphasis on enriched and multivalued mappings; integrating artificial intelligence and machine learning into the design and analysis of methods for challenging problems in optimization and nonlinear analysis; and applications to data science, signal and image recovery, and supply-chain networks.
